Privacy policy

Social Media Manager is a private desktop app used only by pillowforge, on pillowforge's own PC, to manage pillowforge's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What it accesses

With the owner's sign-in, the app reads the owner's own channel and account (their videos, posts and the public comments on them), replies to comments, and publishes or updates the owner's own videos. It acts only when the owner approves each action.

Where data goes

  • Everything the app keeps is stored on the owner's PC. Sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows' credential protection.
  • To draft replies and video titles, comment text and frames from the owner's videos are sent to Anthropic's Claude.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account's security settings.
